Biography

Mark Hanna

Mark Hanna is a writer and entrepreneur who has founded companies and organizations and held leadership positions in education technology, including a vice presidency at a publicly traded Bay Area company. His writing on international politics and social issues has appeared in RealClearPolitics, ZeroHedge, and American Thinker.

His television career included work for NBC and PBS affiliates and CNN in Washington, D.C. He earned bachelor’s degrees in history and political science from Indiana Wesleyan University and a master’s degree in international studies from the University of Utah.

His ministry work includes Youth With A Mission, co-founding The Logos Center in Bratislava, and founding Testimony Networks, which served university graduate students and translated Christian materials into Chinese and Arabic. He served on Young Life’s regional board in Arizona, on Ratio Christi’s international board, and as the founding board chair of Ratio Christi Canada.
