EBenezer

A Globalist’s Christmas Carol

EBenezer, known as EB, has amassed extraordinary wealth and influence as the world’s leading technology mogul. He intends to use both to bring about a global order under his control.

Three spirits take him through the past, present and future, showing him a world shaped by a technocratic elite. In this retelling of A Christmas Carol, EB faces a choice between changing course and reaching for still more power.
